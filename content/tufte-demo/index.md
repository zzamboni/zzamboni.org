## A quieter reading page

{{< newthought >}}Typography sets the pace.{{< /newthought >}} This experiment gives the article a book-like reading measure, restrained headings, and room for commentary beside the text.

{{< sidenote >}}
Numbered notes sit beside the passage on wide screens and remain visible in the reading flow on smaller screens.
{{< /sidenote >}}

The main text can continue without making the reader jump to the bottom of the page.

{{< marginnote >}}
An unnumbered margin note works well for a source, a definition, or a short aside.
{{< /marginnote >}}
