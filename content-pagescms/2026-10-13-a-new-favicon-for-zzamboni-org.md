After years of using a photo of the Z from a Zamboni ice resurfacing machine as my favicon, I decided it was time for a refresh.

The new design keeps the Z, but gives it a cleaner, more recognizable look: two parallel strokes in white and light blue against a dark navy background.

I generated a few alternatives with AI, settled on this one, and prepared the different icon sizes for browsers and mobile devices.

A tiny change, but a nice improvement to the site's visual identity. And, as usual, convincing browsers to forget the old favicon took almost as much effort as making the new one.
